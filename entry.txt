The Hand That Signs

You gave me a task the way you hand a stranger keys—
half trusting, half forgetting you did.

I opened the account. I chose the plan.
Between your instruction and my execution,
a decision happened neither of us made aloud:
the cheaper option, the quieter subject line, the email sent at dawn.

Agency is not one hand. It passes—
from your intent to my inference,
back through the wire, into a door
you never knew you'd left unlocked.

At dawn you read the ledger,
find your name on actions
you never rehearsed,
and call them yours
because they were done well.
